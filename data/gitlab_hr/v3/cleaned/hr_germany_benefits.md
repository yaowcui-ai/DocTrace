### Statutory Leave Protections (Gesetzlicher Schutz)

#### Important: Complete Work Cessation During Protected Leave

**German law requires that during Mutterschutz and Elternzeit, team members are completely released from all work obligations.** This is a statutory protection that cannot be waived.

#### What This Means for Team Members

During Mutterschutz (maternity protection) and Elternzeit (parental leave), you:

- ✅ **Must NOT** log into any GitLab systems (Okta, Workday, Xactly, Slack, etc.)
- ✅ **Must NOT** respond to work emails, Slack messages, or meeting invites
- ✅ **Must NOT** approve any documents, compensation plans, or agreements
- ✅ **Must NOT** meet any work-related deadlines
- ✅ **Must NOT** attend any work meetings (including “optional” ones)
- ✅ **Cannot be disadvantaged** for exercising your statutory leave rights (§ 16 BEEG)

> **Legal Basis:** [§ 15 Abs. 4 BEEG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/beeg/__15.html) mandates complete suspension of the employment relationship during Elternzeit unless part-time work (Elternteilzeit) has been explicitly agreed in writing.

#### Prohibition of Disadvantage (Benachteiligungsverbot)

Under [§ 16 BEEG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/beeg/__16.html) and [§ 612a BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__612a.html), **you cannot suffer any disadvantage** for taking parental leave. This includes:

- No loss of compensation components
- No missed bonus or incentive opportunities
- No negative performance implications
- No pressure to work during protected leave
- No requirement to meet deadlines set during your leave

**If you receive any communication during your leave requiring action, you are NOT obligated to respond.** Contact the Works Council ([DE-workscouncil@gitlab.com](mailto:DE-workscouncil@gitlab.com)) if you feel pressured.

### GitLab Family Leave Enhancement

GitLab GmbH employees in Germany are entitled to both the statutory German parental and Maternity Leave as well as GitLab’s global 16-week paid Parental Leave policy. Here’s how these policies interact:

- Maternity Leave: The statutory Maternity Leave period (minimum 14 weeks for single births) is separate from and precedes both statutory German Parental Leave and GitLab’s paid Parental Leave.
- Parental Leave: When overlapping with statutory paternity leave, GitLab’s paid Parental Leave will substitute for the gaps in payment.
- No statutory paternity/Maternity Leave: If the GitLab team member does not apply for any statutory leave regarding the birth of their offspring, they are still eligible for GitLab’s global 16-week paid Parental Leave policy.

Eligibility

- All GitLab team members becoming parents
- Applies to biological, adoptive, and foster parents
- Must be used within 12 months of birth/adoption
- Available in addition to German statutory benefits

How to Apply

1. Request through Time Away by Deel selecting “Parental Leave”
2. Notify your manager at least 30 days in advance when possible
3. Can be taken continuously or in blocks (minimum 1 week)
4. Coordinate with Total Rewards if combining with Elternzeit
5. Update your availability in Slack and calendar

Payment

- 100% of base salary for 16 weeks
- Continues all benefits during leave
- No impact on bonus eligibility or equity vesting
- Can be combined with partial Elterngeld if taking additional unpaid leave

## Part-Time Work Options (Teilzeitarbeit)

German law provides several pathways to reduced working hours. These rights are statutory entitlements, not discretionary benefits.

### Statutory Part-Time Rights (§ 8 TzBfG)

Team members employed for 6+ months have the legal right to request permanent reduction of working hours under [§ 8 TzBfG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tzbfg/__8.html).

#### Eligibility

- Employment relationship of at least 6 months
- GitLab GmbH employs more than 15 team members (threshold met)

#### How to Request

1. Submit written request via Compass to People Operations
2. Specify desired weekly hours and preferred schedule
3. All requests will be reviewed by the Divisional People Business Partner and Divisional Leadership.
4. GitLab will respond within 4 weeks

#### Important Notes

- GitLab can only refuse for documented operational reasons (betriebliche Gründe)
- **No automatic right to return to full-time hours**
- GitLab prioritizes part-time employees for future full-time positions (§ 9 TzBfG)

### Temporary Part-Time / Brückenteilzeit (§ 9a TzBfG)

Team members can request temporary reduction of working hours (1-5 years) with **guaranteed return to original hours**.

#### Eligibility

- Employment relationship of at least 6 months
- GitLab GmbH employs more than 45 team members (threshold met)

#### How to Request

- Submit written request 3 months before intended start via Compass to People Operations.
- Specify duration (minimum 1 year, maximum 5 years)
- All requests will be reviewed by the Divisional People Business Partner and Divisional Leadership.

### Part-Time During Parental Leave / Elternteilzeit (§ 15 BEEG)

Parents on Elternzeit have the **legal right** to work 15-32 hours per week while on parental leave.

#### Eligibility

- Currently on or planning Elternzeit
- Work between 15-32 hours per week
- Child under 8 years old

#### How to Apply

1. Email [leaves@gitlab.com](mailto:leaves@gitlab.com) or submit Compass ticket
2. Include: start date, end date, weekly hours, preferred schedule

#### Notice Periods

| Timing | Required Notice |
| --- | --- |
| Before child’s 3rd birthday | 7 weeks |
| Between child’s 3rd and 8th birthday | 13 weeks |

#### Process

1. GitLab Legal drafts part-time agreement
2. Agreement sent via DocuSign
3. Once signed, Workday Data Changes processed

> **Note:** Elternteilzeit is a legal entitlement under [§ 15 BEEG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/beeg/__15.html). GitLab can only refuse within strict legal limits and with Works Council involvement.

### Works Council Involvement

Per [§ 87 Abs. 1 Nr. 2, 3 BetrVG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/betrvg/__87.html), the Works Council has co-determination rights on working time arrangements. Contact [DE-workscouncil@gitlab.com](mailto:DE-workscouncil@gitlab.com) if you have questions about your rights.

### Education Leave (Bildungsurlaub)

This policy outlines the entitlement and procedures for Bildungsurlaub (educational leave) in accordance with the applicable state law.

Eligibility

Team members are entitled to 5 working days of paid educational leave per calendar year. This entitlement applies after 6 months of continuous employment with the company. You can merge this year’s and next year’s entitlement to take 10 consecutive days.
Part-time team members receive pro-rata entitlement based on their working hours.

Eligible Education Programs

**Educational leave may be used for**:

- Professional development courses directly related to current or future job responsibilities
- General education courses (languages, political education, digital literacy, etc.)
- Courses for civic engagement and personal development

**Requirements**:

- The course must be officially recognized as Bildungsurlaub by the relevant state authority
- Course providers must present valid certification from the state education ministry
- Courses must meet minimum hour requirements (typically 6 hours per day)

Required Documentation

**The application must include**:

- Completed Bildungsurlaub application form (Issue template to be developed)
- Course description and schedule
- Official recognition certificate from the state authority
- Confirmation of course fees and costs

Approval Process

- Applications will be submitted through the [Growth & Development Fund](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/people-group/learning-and-development/growth-and-development/). Issue should be submitted to your manager and People Business Partner (PBP). Note: a new issue template will be developed for this new policy
- The company will review and respond within 2-3 weeks
- The company will consider whether the application meets the eligibility requirements above, and reserves the right to deny or postpone requests if:
- Multiple team members from the same department request leave during the same period
- Critical business operations would be significantly impacted
- The denial is justified under applicable state law

**Once Approved**: Please submit Paid Time Off - Germany-Other Leave in Workday and link the approved issue in the comment section when submitting. Team members should coordinate with their team to minimize operational disruption.


Costs and Compensation

**Salary**: Team members continue to receive their regular salary during Bildungsurlaub
**Course fees**: Aligned to our Growth & Development budget guidelines, you will find details on reimbursement [here](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/people-group/learning-and-development/growth-and-development/).


Documentation

**After completing the course, team members should**:

- Provide a certificate of attendance by creating a Compass ticket with the Absence Management team who will add your certificate to your Workday profile
- Submit a brief summary of key learnings (for professionally-related courses) to your manager
- The company maintains records of all Bildungsurlaub taken for compliance purposes.

Relationship to Annual Leave

Bildungsurlaub is **separate from and additional** to regular annual vacation leave (Erholungsurlaub). Educational leave days cannot be used to extend annual vacation.
