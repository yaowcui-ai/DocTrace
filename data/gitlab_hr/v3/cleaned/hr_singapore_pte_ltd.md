# GitLab Singapore Pte Ltd

Discover GitLab’s benefits for team members in Singapore

## We’ve Got You Covered

At GitLab, we believe team member benefits should be simple to understand and there when you need them. From life’s big moments to everyday questions, our comprehensive coverage gives you clarity and confidence so you can focus on your work and life.
In addition to the local benefits listed below, all Singapore team members have access to the [company-wide benefits](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/total-rewards/benefits/general-and-entity-benefits/) available to all team members globally.

## Health & Wellbeing

### Medical Insurance

GitLab Singapore team members are enrolled in comprehensive medical insurance benefits through HSBC Insurance.

Coverage & Key Features

- **Hospital and Surgical** - Coverage for inpatient procedures and hospital stays
- **Major Medical** - Protection for significant medical expenses
- **Maternity** - Support for growing families
- **Outpatient General Practitioner** - Everyday medical care when you need it
- **Outpatient Specialist** - Access to specialized medical expertise
- **Dental** - Coverage for oral health needs

See the [GitLab Singapore Benefit Overview](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1OxHW3FxBqSfm6P_cfZ4lZ-WMDHYC27-H/view?usp=sharing) for more details.


How to Access Specialist Care

**Step 1**: Check if your specialist is in the [IHP Panel Clinics Directory](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1FO3ZpkrHGuaqooMNDUrCI62i2JQOW6vE/view?usp=sharing). Please check the IHP App for the most up to date list.

**Step 2**:

- **If your specialist is in the panel**: Contact IHP at +64 6715 9422 before your appointment for cashless arrangement
- **If your specialist is not in the panel**: Obtain a specialist memo with diagnosis and submit with your claim through the IHP portal

Access the IHP Portal to:

- Submit and track claims
- Schedule teleconsultations

- Request Letters of Guarantee
- View your benefit entitlements
- Access the member hotline
- Shop the I-Health Palace marketplace
- Find helpful resources

GitLab provides company-paid Group Term Life Insurance through HSBC Insurance

- **Free Cover Limit (FCL)**: $300,000 (coverage above FCL is subject to underwriting and acceptance).

## Growing Family Leaves

GitLab Singapore combines statutory leave with our global parental leave policy to provide comprehensive support for growing families.

### Maternity Leave

Team members employed for at least 3 months before birth may be eligible for the maternity leaves listed below.

Government-Paid Maternity Leave (16 weeks)

- **1st and 2nd births**: 8 weeks employer-paid + 8 weeks government-paid
- **3rd+ births**: 16 weeks government-paid
- First 8 weeks must be continuous; remaining weeks flexible within 12 months of birth
- Eligible team members receive concurrent [GitLab Parental Leave](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/total-rewards/benefits/general-and-entity-benefits/#parental-leave)

Employment Act Maternity Leave (12 weeks)

- 8 weeks paid + 4 weeks unpaid
- With [GitLab Parental Leave](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/total-rewards/benefits/general-and-entity-benefits/#parental-leave), eligible team members receive up to 16 weeks paid

How to Apply

1. Submit “Parental Leave” request in Workday at least 30 days before leave
2. Send [GPML Declaration Form](https://file.go.gov.sg/gpml1form.pdf) to [leaves@gitlab.com](mailto:leaves@gitlab.com)
3. GitLab handles government reimbursement claims

### Paternity Leave

Team members employed for at least 3 months before due date may receive:

- 4 weeks government-paid paternity leave
- Up to 16 weeks total when combined with [GitLab Parental Leave](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/total-rewards/benefits/general-and-entity-benefits/#parental-leave)
  for eligible team members

How to Apply

1. Submit “Parental Leave” request in Workday
2. Send [GPPL Declaration Form](https://file.go.gov.sg/gpfd1.pdf) to [leaves@gitlab.com](mailto:leaves@gitlab.com)

Learn more about eligibility at the [Ministry of Manpower website](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/leave/paternity-leave).

### Shared Parental Leave

Team members whose child’s date of birth, Estimated Delivery Date (EDD), or Formal Intent to Adopt Date (FIAD) is on or after 1 April 2026 are eligible for **10 weeks** of government-paid SPL, in addition to the existing Maternity and Paternity Leave entitlements above.

- 10 weeks shared between both parents (5 weeks each by default; reallocation available via the LifeSG portal)
- Fully government-paid, capped at SGD 2,500 per week including CPF contributions
- Must be used within 12 months of the child’s birth
- Learn more about eligibility at the [Ministry of Manpower website](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/leave/shared-parental-leave).

How to Apply

1. Provide at least 4 weeks’ notice before commencing a continuous block of SPL
2. Request “Shared Parental Leave” by emailing [leaves@gitlab.com](mailto:leaves@gitlab.com)

- Holiday allowance: Holiday allowance: After 3 months of employment, accrual rates will be based on tenure. Team members are allowed to carry over unused vacation up to the statutory accrual limit based on tenure.

  - 1 year of service: 7 days/year
  - 2 years of service: 8 days/year
  - 3 years of service: 9 days/year
  - 4 years of service: 10 days/year
  - 5 years of service: 11 days/year
  - 6 years of service: 12 days/year
  - 7 years of service: 13 days/year
  - 8 years of service: 14 days/year
